## [Unreleased]

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-13

The first tagged release. What's listed is what changed since the SDK was first published.

### Added

- `RoomSession.send(text:)` and `send(files:text:)`, and `ThreadSession.reply(text:delivery:)` and `send(files:text:delivery:)`, which take text someone typed. It is escaped, so `<b>` shows as typed, and each line stays its own. Blank text, or text over `Limits.message` characters, throws `EmberError` of kind `.invalid` and nothing is sent. `RichText.body(fromPlainText:)` does the escaping.
- Unsent messages are kept across launches. `UnsentMessageStore` is the protocol; `FileUnsentMessageStore` (the default for a real client) and `MemoryUnsentMessageStore` implement it; `UnsentMessage` and `UnsentMessageKey` are what they hold. Room messages and thread replies are both kept, by server, person and room, and are sent again under the same id after a relaunch. `RoomSession.unsentStore` is the session's store, and `RoomSession(client:roomID:around:unsentStore:)` takes one.
- `UnsentMessageStore.discardUnsent(server:userID:)` forgets everything one account left unsent on a server, in every room, for signing out. It drops the saves still waiting for that account, then removes what's kept, in order with the store's other writes, and throws if the store couldn't. `removeAll(server:userID:)` is the store's own part of that; an app calls `discardUnsent`.
- `FileUnsentMessageStore.shared`, the one store on the default folder, which `EmberEnvironment.system()` uses. `FileUnsentMessageStore.init(folder:includedInBackup:)` makes another; `defaultFolder`, `version` and `quarantineLimit` are its constants, and `directory(server:userID:)`, `fileURL(for:)` and `quarantine(server:userID:)` say where an account's files are.
- `UnsentStoreError`, what a store throws. Its `kind` is `.unreadable`, `.corrupt`, `.newerVersion(_:)`, `.unwritable` or `.couldNotRemove`; it carries the `file`, the `underlying` error and, for a damaged file, the messages `recovered` from it and how many were `lost`.
- `RoomSession.unsentState`, one of `.saved`, `.saving` and `.failed(_:)`, and `RoomSession.unsentLoss`, set when a room's file was damaged and some of what it held couldn't be read. Both are observable. A session goes on sending whatever its store does.
- `RoomSession.flushUnsent()`, `Workspace.flushUnsent()` and `EmberClient.flushUnsent()`, which wait until what's unsent is written and throw if it couldn't be. Call `Workspace.flushUnsent()` as the app goes to the background, inside background time of your own.
- `MemoryUnsentMessageStore.failsReads`, `failsWrites` and `failsRemovals`, for testing what an app does when its store fails.
- `RoomSession.sendTimeout` (30 seconds) and `RoomSession.notSent`. A message or reply the room hasn't confirmed by then, on a connection it let in, fails with that text, ready for `retry` or `discard`.
- Async throwing `RoomSession.edit(message:body:)`, `delete(message:)`, `boost(message:content:)` and `removeBoost(id:)`. They return once the room shows the change made, and throw the room's refusal, `.timedOut` after `RoomSession.changeTimeout` (with `changeTimedOut` as its message), or `.unreachable`. A refusal is thrown to whoever asked for the change and isn't put in `notice`.
- `EmberError.kind`, one of `.unreachable`, `.http`, `.undecodable`, `.unreadableFile`, `.invalid`, `.refused` and `.timedOut`, and `EmberError(kind:status:message:field:)`.
- `RoomSession.phase` (`.idle`, `.connecting`, `.admitted`, `.reconnecting`, `.stopped(_:)`), observable.
- `RoomSession.reconnect()` and `ThreadSession.reconnect()`, which connect again after the server stopped the connection, while a lease holds the room. Closing a lease and opening another didn't, while any other lease was held.
- `RoomSession.retryOlder()`, `retryNewer()` and `setAwayFromLatest(_:)`, with `Timeline.olderPageFailure`, `newerPageFailure`, `needsCatchUp`, `isAwayFromLatest` and `Timeline.seqReorder`.
- `RoomSession.pageTimeout` (15 seconds) and `Timeline.pageNotLoaded`.
- `EmberClient.checkServer()`, which answers a `ServerCheck`: the bootstrap and the origin the server answered from after redirects.
- `EmberClient.signOutReason`, a `SignOutReason` of `.userInitiated` or `.sessionEnded`, saying why someone was last signed out.
- `ServerAddress.normalizedOrigin(_:)`, `isLocal(_:)` and `needsHTTPS(_:)`.
- `NetworkPathSource` and `SystemNetworkPath`, which tell sockets when the device has a network. `EmberEnvironment.network` takes one, and `Workspace.isNetworkAvailable` reports it. EmberTesting has `MockNetworkPath`, and `EmberClient.mock` takes one.
- `SystemNetworkPath.shared`, one network monitor for the whole process, started by the first listener and cancelled when the last one leaves. `EmberEnvironment.system()` uses it, so every client made that way, and every one of its sockets, shares one `NWPathMonitor`.
- `EmberTiming.reconnectFloor`, `stablePeriod`, `sidebarRefresh`, `directoryRefresh` and `reconnectDelay()`.
- `BackgroundTime` and `RoomSession.backgroundTime`, which ask the system for time to finish an upload as the app goes to the background.
- `Workspace.thread(_:in:at:)`, which opens a thread without moving its room's page.
- `AvatarStyle.initials(of:atMost:)`.
- `EmberBot`, a client for the server's keyed bot API. It is made from a server address, a room and a bot key, needs no signed-in `EmberClient`, and posts text, HTML or a file to the room and boosts a message. The key is never in its description, its debug output or an error.
- `EmberClient.message(_:in:)`, which fetches one message of a room once.
- `KeychainCookieStorage`, one account's cookies kept in memory and written to a `CookieVault` whenever they change, so two accounts on one server never share a session cookie. `KeychainCookieVault` keeps them in a Keychain item (readable after first unlock, this device only), and `KeychainCookieVault.accounts(service:)` lists the items there; `MemoryCookieVault` is for tests. A vault's calls throw when it can't do them (`KeychainCookieVault.Failure` carries the `OSStatus`), and `read()` answers nil only when nothing is kept. A write the vault refuses is tried again at the next change; after a read that failed, `readFailure` says why and the storage never erases or overwrites the kept item for holding nothing; `syncVault()` reads and writes again now, and answers whether the vault is up to date. `EphemeralCookieStorage` is no longer `final`.
- `RichText.segments(_:mentions:)`, `RichText.mentionRanges(in:mentions:)` and `EditableBody.body(editedTo:mentions:)`, which turn "@Name" typed in plain text into mentions of the people named.
- `SearchSession`, an observable search for a screen to hold: the query being edited, the matches, further pages, recent searches and the state of the search.
- The stand-in server answers the bot API, and lists bot keys to an administrator at `GET /api/account/bots`. `Tools/mock-server/check.mjs` checks both.
- README sections on sending plain text, unsent messages and Retry, changes that throw, signing out and why, network status, search, one message, bots, and app extensions and widgets, each sample run by a test. A compatibility table names the upstream Ember commit the SDK was built against.
- A CI workflow that runs `swift test` and the stand-in server check on macOS.
- `ManualClock.sleeperCount(dueWithin:)`, how many waiting sleeps a move of that length would wake, so a test can wait for a timer to be sleeping before it moves the clock.
- `EmberEnvironment`, what a client runs on: its transport, socket connector, cookies, timing, network path, `UnsentMessageStore` and `BackgroundTime`, each a `var`. `EmberEnvironment.system(cookies:)` is the real thing: one URLSession for HTTP and sockets, `SystemNetworkPath.shared`, `FileUnsentMessageStore.shared` and `BackgroundTime.system`. Change what you need on it, such as `transport`, and the rest stays real.
- `EmberClient.init(origin:environment:)`, and `EmberClient.environment`, `unsentStore` and `backgroundTime`. A `RoomSession` keeps unsent messages in its client's `unsentStore` unless given its own, and starts with its client's `backgroundTime`.
- `EmberEnvironment.mock(transport:connector:cookies:timing:network:unsentStore:backgroundTime:)` in EmberTesting, for a test with a transport or connector of its own: `EmberClient(origin: url, environment: .mock(transport: mine))`. `EmberClient.mock` takes `unsentStore` and `backgroundTime` too, in memory and `.none` unless given.
- Making and changing rooms. `EmberClient.createRoom(_:)` takes a `NewRoom` (a name, an icon and a `RoomAccess` of `.open` or `.closed(memberIDs:)`), `changeRoom(_:_:)` a `RoomChanges` where nil leaves a part as it is, and `deleteRoom(_:)` deletes a room or DM. `newRoomForm()` answers the people a new room can have (`NewRoomForm`), and `roomSettings(_:)` a `RoomSettings`: the room, whether you can administer it, and everyone as a `RoomPerson` saying who's a member. `Workspace.createRoom(_:)`, `changeRoom(_:_:)` and `deleteRoom(_:)` do the same and show the change in the sidebar at once.
- Sidebar order. `Workspace.favoriteRooms` and `moveFavorites(fromOffsets:toOffset:)` order your starred rooms; `roomsInSharedOrder`, `sharedOrder`, `moveSharedRooms(fromOffsets:toOffset:)` and `clearSharedOrder()` order shared rooms for everyone, for administrators. A move shows at once and goes back, with a `notice`, if the server refuses it. `EmberClient.setFavoriteOrder(_:)` and `setSharedOrder(_:)` are the calls underneath, and `RoomList.inSharedOrder(_:sharedOrder:)` the ordering.
- `Workspace.invitation`, the sidebar's welcome card, and `Workspace.dismissInvitation()` and `EmberClient.dismissInvitation()`, which dismiss it for good.
- Link previews. `EmberClient.linkPreview(for:)` answers a `LinkPreview` (title, address, image and description) or nil when the page has none. `RoomSession.send(text:previews:)` and `send(body:plainText:previews:)`, and `ThreadSession.reply(text:delivery:previews:)` and `reply(body:plainText:delivery:previews:)`, send previews after the message as the web composer does; `RichText.previewAttachment(_:)` is the markup for one.
- Your profile. `EmberClient.profile()` answers a `ProfileAnswer` holding a `UserProfile`; `changeProfile(_:)` takes a `ProfileChanges` (name, email address, bio, password and the current password, nil leaving each as it is), `changeAvatar(_:filename:contentType:variant:)` gives you a picture of up to `avatarByteLimit` bytes, and `removeAvatar()` takes it away. `AvatarVariant` is the small copy shown in lists, which the SDK makes with `AvatarVariant(of:)` when it isn't given one. `Workspace.changeProfile(_:)`, `changeAvatar(_:contentType:variant:)` and `removeAvatar()` do the same and show your new name, bio and picture at once. A `ProfileChanges` never prints its passwords.
- People and bans. `EmberClient.person(_:)` answers a `PersonDetails`: the person and, for an administrator, their email address. `ban(_:)` and `unban(_:)` answer the person as changed. `Workspace.ban(_:)` and `unban(_:)` show it in `people` at once, and `Workspace.bannedPeople` lists everyone banned, by name.
- `Secret`, text that lets someone in, such as a sign-in link's id or an invitation's link. It prints as `<redacted>`, isn't `Encodable`, and gives its text only as `revealed`.
- Signing in with a sign-in link. `SignInLink(_:)` reads a pasted link into its `origin` and `transferID`, and `EmberClient.signIn(transferID:)` signs in with it.
- Resetting a password. `EmberClient.askForPasswordReset(emailAddress:turnstileToken:)` emails a code and `resetPassword(emailAddress:code:newPassword:)` sets the new password with it, answering who's signed in. `Bootstrap.turnstileSiteKey` says when the server wants a Turnstile token with the request.
- Inviting people by email, for administrators. `EmberClient.invite(emailAddresses:)` takes up to `EmailInvitation.mostAtOnce` addresses and answers an `InvitationOutcome` for each: `.member` for someone already here, or `.invited` with the `EmailInvitation` and, when the email couldn't be sent, its link as a `Secret` to pass on yourself. `invitations()` lists the pending ones, `resendInvitation(_:)` sends one again (a `ResentInvitation`), and `revokeInvitation(_:)` withdraws one. `EmailInvitation.delivery` is `.sent`, `.refused(_:)` or `.unknown(_:)`.
- EmberTesting fixtures for all of the above, in `Fixtures+Rooms.swift` and `Fixtures+People.swift`: `Fixtures.newRoomFormJSON`, `roomSettingsJSON`, `sharedOrderJSON`, `linkPreview`, `linkPreviewJSON`, `invitation`, `sidebarJSON(invitation:sharedOrder:)`, `profile`, `profileJSON`, `personJSON`, `changedPersonJSON`, `emailInvitation`, `accountInvitationsJSON` and `transferID`.
- The stand-in server answers every route above: rooms and their settings, both sidebar orders, the invitation card, link previews (a canned one for `example.com`), profiles and avatars, people and bans, sign-in links, password resets and email invitations. It prints each reset code and invitation link it would have emailed, and lists them at `GET /mock/outbox`. `Tools/mock-server/check.mjs` checks them all.
- Live tests for every route above, in `Tests/EmberLiveTests`. They ban people, reset a password and reorder the sidebar for everyone, so they run only against the stand-in on this machine: a loopback `EMBER_TEST_ORIGIN` that answers `GET /mock/outbox`. Against any other server they are skipped.
- `UploadSource.Disposition`, what the SDK does with a file once it's done with it. `.preserve` only reads it. `.deleteWhenFinished` deletes it once it's posted, discarded or refused as too large, or when the session is shut down, and then the folder it was in if that's left empty (never the temporary directory itself); one that failed and can still be retried is kept. `UploadSource.temporary(fileURL:name:contentType:preview:)` makes a source for a copy the app made to send.
- `ReplyDelivery`, `.threadOnly` or `.threadAndRoom`: whether a thread reply shows in the room as well.
- `SearchHistory`, `.record` or `.skip`: whether a search joins the person's recent searches.
- `SearchQuery(_:)`, made from the text alone, and `inRoom(_:)`, `from(_:)`, `involvingMe()`, `havingLinks()`, `havingFiles()` and `inThreads()`, each answering a copy with one more filter, as in `SearchQuery("launch").inRoom(id).havingFiles()`.

### Changed

- Options that were bare booleans have names. Nothing sent to the server or saved to disk changes, and unsent messages saved before still read.
  - `UploadSource.init` takes `disposition:`, `.preserve` unless given, in place of `isTemporary:`. Write `UploadSource.temporary(…)` where you wrote `isTemporary: true`.
  - `ThreadSession.reply` and `send(files:)`, `EmberClient.upload(_:in:clientMessageId:parentId:delivery:progress:)` and `NewUpload.init` take `delivery:`, a `ReplyDelivery`, in place of `alsoSend:`, and `PendingMessage.delivery` and `UnsentMessage.delivery` replace their `alsoSend`. `.threadAndRoom` is what `true` was.
  - `EmberClient.search(_:sort:cursor:history:)` and `SearchSession.run(history:)` take a `SearchHistory` in place of `record:`. `.record` is what `true` was.
- Ids have their own types: `UserID`, `RoomID`, `MessageID`, `ClientMessageID`, `BoostID`, `UploadID` and `FileID`, each conforming to the new `EmberIdentifier` protocol, in place of `String` (and of the `UserID` and `RoomID` typealiases) across EmberCore, EmberSDK and EmberTesting. Each wraps its string unchanged, is made from a literal, an interpolation or `init(_:)`, reads back as `rawValue`, describes itself as that string, sorts by it, and is a bare string in JSON, also as a dictionary key; nothing sent or saved changes. A `String` variable needs wrapping, as `MessageID(text)`. `RoomSession.send` and `ThreadSession.reply` return a `ClientMessageID`. EmberSDK adds `UserID` forms of EmberRichText's mention calls, such as `ComposeSegment.mention(_:name:)` and `EditableBody(body:mentionNames:)`.
- `Role`, `UserStatus`, `RoomKind`, `Involvement` and `Bootstrap.Setup` keep a value this build doesn't know as `.unknown(String)` instead of reading it as a fallback, and write it back unchanged. They conform to the new `OpenEnum` protocol, which replaces `FallbackDecodable`, and the `fallback` members are gone. A `switch` over one of them needs a case for `.unknown`. `init(rawValue:)` still answers nil for an unknown value; `init(_:)` keeps it, and `isKnown` says which it is. An unknown value answers no to every question: not an administrator or a bot, not active, neither open nor direct, neither hidden nor muted, and not waiting for setup.
- `EmberBot.post(text:)` now sends plain text, escaped as `RoomSession.send(text:)` escapes it, and throws `.invalid` for blank or over-long text. Use `post(html:)` to send markup as it is.
- `EmberError.isUnreachable` now reads `kind`. It answers as before for every error the SDK made before.
- `Workspace.sharedRooms`, `directRooms`, `suggestedPeople` and `unreadRooms` are stored, and sorted once per change, instead of being worked out on every read.
- A retried upload goes on from the parts the server already took. A part is tried again with backoff after no answer, 408, 429 or a 5xx.
- `EmberSDK` now depends on `EmberRichText`.
- `EmberClient.init(origin:transport:connector:cookies:timing:)` and `init(origin:transport:connector:cookies:timing:network:)` are gone. Write `EmberClient(origin:environment:)`: `.system()` with `transport` and `connector` changed for an app's own, or `.mock(transport:connector:…)` in tests. The first one's sockets took the network to be always there; `.system()` has `SystemNetworkPath`, and `.mock()` a `MockNetworkPath` that's there until a test takes it away.
- `UnsentMessageStore` is no longer `@MainActor`, and `messages(for:)`, `save(_:for:)` and `removeAll(server:userID:)` throw. It refines `AnyObject` and `Sendable`: `save` and `removeAll` are called off the main actor, one at a time for each store, and `messages(for:)` on it. `FileUnsentMessageStore` and `MemoryUnsentMessageStore` are safe from any thread, and one store serves every session on a client. A store of your own needs `throws` on its three calls and its `@MainActor` taken off.
- Unsent messages are written off the main actor, on a queue the SDK keeps for each store, so a slow or stuck write holds up only that store's writes and never one of Swift's shared threads. Each store has one writer, which makes the store's calls one at a time and in order, and keeps only the newest list waiting for each room. A session takes a list as saved only once its write succeeded; one that failed is kept and tried again at the next change, when the room is opened or resumed, at `Workspace.start()` and at a flush.
- `FileUnsentMessageStore` keeps each room's messages in a file named by a hash of the room, in a folder named by a hash of the server and person, so every name is the same length whatever the ids. The file is an envelope holding a version, its key and the messages. A file in the format before is still read, and is moved to the new format at the room's next save, so nothing saved is lost; reading never writes. The folder is left out of backups unless the store is made with `includedInBackup: true`. Two stores or two processes writing the same room each write the whole list, and the last one wins: keep one writer for an account.
- `Workspace.connection` and `RoomSession.status` are a `ConnectionStatus`, and `RoomSession.Phase.stopped(_:)` holds a `ConnectionStatus.StopReason`, in place of `ReconnectingSocket.Status` and `ReconnectingSocket.StopReason`. The cases are the same: write `ConnectionStatus` where you wrote `ReconnectingSocket.Status`.
- `MockSocketConnection.sentFrames` is a list of `SentFrame` in place of `ClientFrame`. Each has the frame's `type`, its `fields` and its `text`, and `string(_:)` or a subscript reads one field: `frame.string("body")` is a `message.create`'s body, and `frame.string("before")` the message a `history.before` pages back from. A frame that isn't a JSON object with a `type` is left out.
- `Timeline`, `ThreadTimeline` and `SidebarState` are read-only to an app: their properties and `Timeline.message(_:)` and `parentText(of:)` are public, but their initialisers and the methods that change them are not. `RoomSession`, `ThreadSession` and `Workspace` make the changes, as before; `RoomSession.retryOlder()`, `retryNewer()` and `setAwayFromLatest(_:)` stand in for `Timeline.retryOlderPage()`, `retryNewerPage()` and `setAwayFromLatest(_:)`, and `Timeline.olderPageFailed(_:)`, `newerPageFailed(_:)` and `ThreadTimeline.olderPageFailed(_:)` are the sessions' own to call. `ThreadTimeline.notice` can be read but not set; `ThreadSession.notice` is the one to show.

### Removed

- The socket protocol, which an app never builds or reads, is no longer public: `ClientFrame`, `ServerFrame`, `WorkspaceFrame`, `TimelinePage`, `RoomSocket` and `SocketClose`; `ReconnectingSocket`; and `EmberClient.workspaceSocket()`, `roomSocket(request:)`, `workspaceSocketRequest()` and `roomSocketRequest(roomID:visible:since:around:)`. `Workspace` and `RoomSession` hold the sockets. A test plays the server with `MockSocketConnector`, `Fixtures.RoomFrames` and `Fixtures.WorkspaceFrames`, and reads what the client sent with `sentFrames`. A close code is its number, such as 4003 for `SocketClose.revoked`. `Workspace.isNetworkAvailable` replaces `ReconnectingSocket.isNetworkAvailable`.
- The bodies of calls `EmberClient` makes itself are no longer public: `PasswordSignIn`, `GuestSignIn`, `SignedIn`, `NewDirect`, `StartedDirect`, `SearchBody`, `SearchQuery.body(sort:)`, `SearchQuery.queryString(sort:cursor:)` and `FavoriteGuess`. Use `signIn(emailAddress:password:)`, `signIn(name:)`, `me()`, `startDirect(with:)`, `search(_:sort:cursor:history:)` and `Workspace.setFavorite(_:roomID:)`.
- `RoomSession.edit(_:body:)`, `delete(_:)`, `boost(_:content:)` and `removeBoost(_:)`, which didn't wait, answered a `Bool` and wrote a failure into `notice`. Use `edit(message:body:)`, `delete(message:)`, `boost(message:content:)` and `removeBoost(id:)`, which wait for the room and throw.
- `SearchQuery.init(q:roomID:from:withMe:hasLink:hasFile:inThread:)`. Write `SearchQuery("launch")` and add filters with `inRoom(_:)`, `from(_:)` and the rest, or set its properties.
- `UploadSource.isTemporary`. Read `disposition`.

### Fixed

- `Workspace.suggestedPeople` listed people who are banned, deactivated or suspended. It now leaves out anyone the directory has as not active, as the web sidebar does, as soon as they're banned and again once they're let back in. `RoomList.suggestedPeople(_:rooms:people:)` takes the directory to do it, and `RoomList.isSuggestable(_:people:)` is the rule.
- A client on a transport other than `URLSessionHTTPTransport` kept unsent messages in memory only, losing them when the app quit, and asked for no background time while a file went up. Both now come from the client's environment, whatever its transport.
- `RoomSession.loadOlder()` and `loadNewer()` called while the room was reconnecting did nothing, so a view that asks once at the top of a page never asked again and older paging stopped until the room was reopened. The page now goes once the room lets the socket in, unless a jump or a new page has replaced the messages it was for.
- A page of older or newer messages the room never answered left paging stuck with no failure, and one on its way when the socket dropped was forgotten without one, so no retry showed and scrolling up loaded nothing again until the room was reopened. Either now fails as a refused page does, `retryOlder()` and `retryNewer()` ask again, and paging works after any failure. A thread's older page does the same, with its `notice`.
- A message or reply sent while the room was reconnecting stayed pending until the connection came back, however long. It's now timed from when it was sent, and fails with `RoomSession.notSent` after `sendTimeout`; one that failed before it was written isn't sent when the connection returns, and Retry asks the room first as before.
- An async edit, delete, boost or boost removal asked for while the room was reconnecting threw `.unreachable` at once. They now wait for the room to let the socket in, timed from when they were asked for, and throw `.timedOut` after `changeTimeout`; one that timed out is never sent.
- A message sent while the socket was reconnecting could be lost: it wasn't counted as busy, and leaving the room closed the socket under it. An unsent message now keeps the socket open until it's confirmed, failed or discarded.
- Nothing was saved before a room's first welcome. A room made by `Workspace` now saves what's typed from the start.
- A list of unsent messages that couldn't be written was taken as saved and not written again until it changed, so a full disk or a locked file lost what was unsent at the next launch, and a file that couldn't be deleted brought back a message already sent or discarded. The write is now tried again until it succeeds, and `unsentState` says while it hasn't.
- A file of unsent messages that couldn't be opened, as before the device is first unlocked, read as nothing saved and was then written over. It is now left as it is and read again at the room's next welcome, open, resume or flush, and what it holds joins what was sent meanwhile.
- One damaged message, or a file that wasn't JSON, lost everything unsent in its room without a word. The file is now moved to a `Quarantine` folder beside it (the newest `quarantineLimit` are kept), every message that can still be read is restored, and `unsentLoss` says how many couldn't be. A file written by a newer version of the SDK is left alone and never written over.
- Unsent messages that couldn't be removed at sign-out stayed, with nothing said. `discardUnsent` now throws, and a removal that failed is tried again before any later save for that account.
- A save still on its way when an account's unsent messages were removed could be written afterwards, if it failed and was tried again. It is now dropped.
- `flushUnsent()` on a room, a workspace or a client also waited for saves handed over after it was called, and couldn't be stopped. It now waits only for the work handed in before it, and throws `CancellationError` when its task is cancelled.
- Reading a damaged file again and again filled the quarantine with identical copies. A copy the same as the newest one there is now skipped.
- Long ids or a long server address made a file name longer than the file system takes, and nothing was saved for that room. Every name is now 64 characters.
- Someone's status heard over the socket while the people directory was loading was undone when the load finished, so someone just banned or deactivated could go on showing as active. What the socket said during a load is now kept over the load's answer.
- An async edit timed out when the server's sanitising left the body as it was, or stored a different body from the one sent.
- One message the SDK couldn't read lost the whole frame it came in; it is now left out on its own. A message's unreadable optional parts are dropped one by one.
- A message that arrived out of order went at the end; it now goes in its place. A change heard twice is ignored, and a gap that lasts three changes reconnects to fetch what was missed.
- After a long outage, the newest page replaced the window someone had scrolled up to. A welcome that overlaps the window is now merged in.
- A failed older or newer page was asked for again and again. It now waits for a retry, a jump or a reconnect.
- A 4001 close reconnected at once, and backoff reset on the first frame heard. A 4001 now waits about `reconnectFloor`, and backoff resets only after `stablePeriod` open.
- An upgrade refused with a 4xx other than 401, 408 or 429 retried forever. It now stops with `StopReason.refused(status:)`.
- A late 401 from a replaced session signed the new one out.
- Origins were compared in more than one form; one form, `normalizedOrigin`, is now used for the Origin header, socket addresses and same-origin checks. A bare private address defaulted to `https`.
- A socket waiting to reconnect kept using up its backoff while the device had no network, and didn't try again as soon as the network came back.
- `SystemNetworkPath` made a new `NWPathMonitor` and dispatch queue on every `availability()` call: one a socket, and another each time a socket restarted. A `SystemNetworkPath` and its copies now share one monitor among all their listeners; someone who starts listening hears the current availability at once, and a value that hasn't changed isn't repeated.
- Opening a room normally after a search jump stayed on the page around that message.
- A file that couldn't be read was reported as the server being unreachable.
- The stand-in server's search took time that grew with the square of a room's length. It is now linear.
- Retry on a message that failed as not sent could post it twice, as the room stores a create twice. `retry` now asks with `message.check` first and sends again only when the room says it's missing, and failed texts are asked about after each reconnect. The stand-in server no longer de-duplicates a create, so it behaves as the real one does.
- A thread reply held behind a file, or sent while a check on it was on its way, could be posted twice: the check came back saying the reply was missing and created it again. A reply is now remembered as created once it's sent, as a room message is.
- A session ended that a room socket found (closed as revoked, or its upgrade refused 401) left `signOutReason` as `.userInitiated` once the app signed out. It is now `.sessionEnded` before the app hears, and a sign-out someone asked for still says `.userInitiated`.
- The SDK's own tests of reconnecting, timeouts and typing failed now and then on a busy machine: they moved the clock before the timer they meant to fire was sleeping. They now wait for that sleep first.
